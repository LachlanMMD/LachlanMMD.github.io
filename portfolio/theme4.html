Theme 4' • Brugergrænsefladeudvikling/Portfolie Eksamen

Theme 4 focused on the connection between design and implementation. The theme explored how interactive interfaces are built and refined through development. Although I was unable to participate fully due to illness, many of the skills covered in the theme became relevant later during the development of this examination portfolio.

Challenge

Theme 4 focused on interface development and the transition from design to implementation.
Instead of presenting incomplete work, I have used this section to reflect on how many of the skills from the theme were applied while building this portfolio.

Process

I spent more time on the design and planning stages of this portfolio than I have on any of my previous projects combined, spending the majority of my time creating wireframes, mockups and prototypes before writing a single line of code. Testing and user interactions were much more deliberate and structured, helping me strip back a lot of the unnecessary design elements that I have a tendency to add in my projects.

I had initially planned a much more complex design inspired by Alice in Wonderland, through the looking glass, but through testing externally and internally, I found that the whimsical design elements were distracting and made it difficult to navigate the site. I ended up simplifying the design significantly, focusing on clear typography, a simple color palette and a straightforward layout.

Solution

The result is the portfolio website you are currently viewing. It brings together the projects from the semester and presents them in a single responsive website. The site includes shared navigation components, interactive elements, responsive layouts and individual pages for each theme, allowing visitors to explore both the final projects and the work behind them.

Reflection

Although I missed most of Theme 4, building this portfolio helped me develop many of the same technical skills. It also showed me how much more confident I have become with HTML, CSS and problem solving compared to the beginning of the semester. If I were to revisit the original Theme 4 project, I would approach it with a much stronger understanding of front end development than I had at the time.
